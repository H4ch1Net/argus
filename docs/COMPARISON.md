# Argus vs the reference project

Argus started from [`bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view`](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view)
(MIT, code only). This page compares the two feature by feature, as of the
reference's commit `95fa816` (October 2026), and says why each gap is either
closed, left out on purpose, or deferred.

How the reference was read: its source, `README.md`, and `DATA_SOURCES.md`.
Endpoints, parameters and field names for everything ported come from its
working code. **None of the new upstreams was reachable from the build
environment** (the npm registry and every data host were blocked by policy), so
each is "per the reference implementation, not live-tested here". The first
live run on Kali or a phone is the real check; `argus health` lists which feeds
the proxy can serve.

Legend: **Have** (Argus had it before this round), **Added** (ported in this
round, the ctOS round: commits after `52cd673` up to `e3f138b`), **Partial** (a narrower
version), **Out** (excluded by the guardrails in `CLAUDE.md`, or not real
data), **Deferred** (fits the guardrails, not built yet).

## Out or deferred, and why

- **Voice control** (and the AI HUD summary that comes from the voice stack): Out. Voice is outside the project's scope by a `CLAUDE.md` guardrail.
- **Mapillary street level**: Deferred, parked for the owner's decision. It needs a token in the browser (conflicts with "no secrets in client code") and its imagery shows people.
- **Traffic simulation**: Out. The reference's vehicles are fabricated; only TomTom's real flow measurement was ported.
- **Wind from GFS / ECMWF GRIB**: not ported. A server-side GRIB decoder dependency could not be installed here; the Wind layer uses Open-Meteo's current wind grid instead (Partial: no forecast timeline).
- **Live Traffic NSW cameras**: not ported. Its image host serves stills only to a spoofed browser User-Agent; Argus names itself to every upstream and does not impersonate a browser.
- **DelDOT cameras**: not ported. HLS video only, no stills; a video relay would be a new subsystem, and redistribution beyond personal viewing is unconfirmed.
- **InciWeb incident lookup**: not ported (an undocumented POST); WFIGS perimeters cover the map.
- **WebUSB SDR, camera video (HLS), hosted scene sharing, the MCP server, the Nepal flood scene**: Deferred.

## What Argus has that the reference does not

- **Runs on the phone and in a terminal.** A phone-first shell (bottom sheet, touch and pen, Around Me, compass point-at-sky), installable PWA, thermal ladder, WebGL context-loss recovery; and a full terminal version (`argus tui`) with the same layers, cards and commands, usable over SSH and in Termux. Android can run the whole stack on the phone itself (`scripts/install-termux.sh`).
- **A proxy that is the security boundary.** Every feed goes through one allowlisted proxy: keys never reach the browser, each feed is pinned to its paths (and most new feeds to their query shape), metered APIs sit behind a rate and credit governor, slow or rate-limited feeds are cached with stale-on-error, and local-only feeds cannot be pointed at third-party hosts.
- **Internet-infrastructure telemetry.** Live BGP updates (RIPE RIS), the Certificate Transparency firehose, Shodan exposure density, and a passive OSINT console (`query` / `correlate` an IP, domain or ASN through RIPEstat and Shodan), all scriptable with `--json`.
- **Time scrubber** over the in-memory history of every mover layer.
- **A scriptable CLI** (`argus quakes | flights | military | storms | launches | sats | fires | geocode | route | measure | bgp | ct | query | correlate | health`).

## Guardrails that shaped the port

- No people: civil aircraft owners are never shown; adsbdb's `registered_owner*` fields are dropped on parse and the adsb.lol trace's per-point details are never read; the GTFS-RT license plate is never decoded into output; OSM layers map facilities, not individuals; cockpit news is searched by a place name derived from coordinates, never free text.
- No analysis of imagery: traffic-camera stills are displayed as published, on request, and nothing processes them; the tracking boxes are drawn from feed positions, never from pixels.
- No active traffic at targets: every new feed is a public index or a public file read through the proxy; "My receiver" reads your own 1090 and 978 MHz decoders on your own network.
- Terms kept: adsbdb route data is shown at run time only; Google News RSS is used for personal, non-commercial viewing; every source is listed in TOOLS > DATA CREDITS.

## Round 3 additions (this branch, beyond the ctOS round)

Built but not live-tested (no network here); every new upstream is pinned in the proxy and marked "per the provider's documentation, not live-tested here".

- **Public webcams** (new `webcams` layer): a category-aware layer over Windy Webcams v3 (keyed), the US National Park Service (keyed), NASA EPIC (keyless, Earth from DSCOVR) and a small curated observatory catalogue, in 15 categories (traffic, city, beach, harbor, mountain, weather, airport, park, wildlife, rail, campus, construction, observatory, space, other), each with its own glyph and a VIEW filter. Stills load only when a card opens.
- **More traffic cameras**: all 12 Caltrans districts, NYC DOT and Singapore LTA (keyless), WSDOT and ten 511-platform states (keyed, offered only once their key is set), plus camera sub-kinds (ramp, bridge, tunnel, pass, border) on the card, in search and as a VIEW filter.
- **Context layers**: aurora (NOAA SWPC OVATION, with a Kp readout), air quality (Open-Meteo / CAMS US AQI, with an AIR readout), day/night terminator with NASA Black Marble night lights, Tor relays (Onionoo), and GDELT news events (three fixed themes only, never free text).
- **Real sensor filters**: NVG, FLIR (four palettes) and CRT are proper post-process effects now, not tints, each compile-checked in WebGL2; NVG/FLIR raise the Intel HUD.
- **More Earth models** (VIEW > BASEMAP / EARTH): Sentinel-2 cloudless, NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble, OpenTopoMap, plus sun lighting, atmosphere, stars and relief exaggeration.
- **Settings** (the SET cell, or the comma key) and a **Setup tab**: quality, frame rate, resolution, detail, units, clock, coordinates, interface size, start view; feed and GPU/font status; API keys entered in-app and saved to the proxy machine's keys file (never the browser) over a loopback-only endpoint; a proxy-address box for phone browsers.
- **Saved places / your own landmarks** (a `myplaces` layer and TOOLS > MY PLACES), **watch areas** (geofence alerts), a **situation-room tour**, and **compare** (pin up to three contacts with live stats and their closest approach).
- **Interactive blob tracking**: closest point of approach between the target and nearby contacts, conflicts flagged in red on the map, in the widget and in the contacts list with a notice; motion leader lines; the widget is clickable with range rings.
- **Android app** (`android/`): an installable APK that runs the proxy on the phone (nodejs-mobile) and shows the globe full screen, plus an Android Auto navigation map on a new car web shell (`shell-car/`), built and published by a GitHub Actions workflow. Not built or run here (no Android SDK or network); the first CI run is the real test.
