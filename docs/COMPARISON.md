# Argus vs the reference project

Argus started from [`bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view`](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view)
(MIT, code only). This page compares the two feature by feature, as of the
reference's commit `95fa816` (October 2026), and says why each gap is either
closed, left out on purpose, or deferred.

How the reference was read: its source, `README.md`, and `DATA_SOURCES.md`.
Endpoints, parameters and field names for the layers ported in this round come
from its working code. **None of the new upstreams was reachable from the build
environment** (outbound access was blocked), so each is "per the reference
implementation, not live-tested here". The first live run on Kali or a phone is
the real check; `argus health` lists which feeds the proxy can serve.

Legend: **Have** (Argus had it before this round), **Added** (ported now),
**Partial** (a narrower version), **Out** (excluded by the guardrails in
`CLAUDE.md`), **Deferred** (fits the guardrails, not built yet).

## What Argus has that the reference does not

- **Runs on the phone and in a terminal.** A phone-first shell (bottom sheet, touch and pen, Around Me, compass point-at-sky), installable PWA, thermal ladder, WebGL context-loss recovery; and a full terminal version (`argus tui`) with the same layers, cards and commands, usable over SSH and in Termux. Android can run the whole stack on the phone itself (`scripts/install-termux.sh`).
- **A proxy that is the security boundary.** Every feed goes through one allowlisted proxy: keys never reach the browser, each feed is pinned to its paths, metered APIs sit behind a rate and credit governor, slow or rate-limited feeds are cached with stale-on-error, and local-only feeds cannot be pointed at third-party hosts.
- **Internet-infrastructure telemetry.** Live BGP updates (RIPE RIS), the Certificate Transparency firehose, Shodan exposure density, and a passive OSINT console (`query` / `correlate` an IP, domain or ASN through RIPEstat and Shodan), all scriptable with `--json`.
- **Time scrubber** over the in-memory history of every mover layer.
- **A scriptable CLI** (`argus quakes | flights | military | storms | launches | sats | fires | bgp | ct | query | correlate | health`).

## Guardrails that shaped the port

- No people: civil aircraft owners are never shown; the GTFS-RT license plate is never decoded into output; OSM layers map facilities, not individuals.
- No analysis of imagery: traffic-camera stills are displayed as published, on request, and nothing processes them.
- No active traffic at targets: every new feed is a public index or a public file read through the proxy; "My receiver" reads your own decoder on your own network.
